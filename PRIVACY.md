# Privacy

Spammish's default desktop version processes Gmail locally on your computer. It contacts Google's OAuth and Gmail endpoints to authorize access, refresh/revoke credentials, inspect the existing Inbox automatically after connection and new mail thereafter, create The Abyss label if missing, and move high-confidence matches by changing labels.

Subject, sender, and bounded message body content are processed in memory. Spammish does not store email bodies, load remote email images, fetch attachments, click message links, or transmit message content to an AI provider or maintainer. The local accounts file holds each connected email address, processing cursor, state, temporary message IDs/page checkpoints during the initial Inbox sweep, and a separate OS-encrypted refresh credential for each account. The first multi-account upgrade migrates the old single-account file without changing its paused/enabled state or sweep progress. Temporary sweep IDs are dropped after completion. It also retains a confirmed-move count, at most 50 recent decision records (message ID, time, score and feature codes; no subject or body), crash-recovery move intent, and account-specific hashed message/sender receipts and correction evidence. These hashes are salted per account; they are not anonymous to someone with access to that account file. Recent subjects/senders are fetched transiently from Gmail when you ask to review them. Desktop metadata is not encrypted by the app; tokens use OS encryption. Protect your OS login and disk. No telemetry or analytics is included.

Candidate messages trigger bounded Gmail history searches for prior outbound communication, received mail, Spam labels and thread context. This is on-demand relationship hydration, not a copy of your entire mailbox or Contacts. URLs are inspected as text, never visited. Gmail Spam labels do not prove who marked the message spam. A move to The Abyss by another Gmail client is observable feedback, not proof of a particular user's action. Automatic Spammish moves do not become rejection evidence.

The loopback server uses the same engine. Its SQLite state, including tokens and metadata, is encrypted with the locally generated `.env` vault key. Anyone possessing both can decrypt it. Disconnect removes the selected saved account and feedback, retaining only its contribution to the anonymous aggregate counter. SQLite/filesystem backups may retain previous encrypted state; disconnect is not forensic erasure.

The Gmail `gmail.modify` grant technically allows broader actions than Spammish performs. The published default code does not send, reply, trash, or delete mail. Classification is conservative but may make mistakes. Review The Abyss when needed and use Rescue or move a message back to Inbox using Gmail. This protects the exact sender and retracts its rejection evidence.

Pause stops the selected account; the tray’s Pause all accounts control and Quit stop all processing. Disconnect clears only the selected account’s local credential and attempts revocation through Google. If offline revocation fails, remove the grant manually in your Google Account's third-party connections. Uninstalling alone may leave the OS account file or Google grant; disconnect first.

These statements describe the default source in this repository. Modified versions and forks can behave differently and are their operators' responsibility. Google's own services are governed by Google's privacy policy.
