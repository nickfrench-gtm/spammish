Into The Abyss.
Out of your way.

The Abyss isn’t deletion. Mail stays in Gmail; use Rescue when Spammish gets it wrong.

The open-source version inspects message text locally and changes Gmail labels. It preserves read and unread status.

It never sends, replies, deletes, clicks email links, or unsubscribes. Gmail access is required to move messages, and mail may appear briefly before the next check.
