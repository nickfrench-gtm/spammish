Waitlist privacy

Updated October 1, 2026.

What you share

The Cloud waitlist collects your email address, the time you join, and your consent to Cloud launch updates. It does not connect to Gmail or collect mailbox content.

Why we keep it

We use the waitlist to send news about Spammish Cloud’s launch. No marketing email is sent automatically by this website. Joining does not create an account or start a subscription.

Storage and access

Entries are stored privately on the site’s Fly.io storage volume. The list is not exposed through a public endpoint. Only the operator can access it. Fly.io may process request metadata to operate the site. We use a temporary hash of your network address in memory to limit abusive submissions; it is not saved with your email.

Your choices

To remove your entry, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile. Do not post your email in a public issue. We retain entries until you ask for removal or the Cloud launch waitlist is retired.

Open-source app

The self-hosted application has its own privacy documentation. Cloud’s service terms, pricing, and privacy details will be provided before it becomes available.
