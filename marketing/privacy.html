Waitlist privacy

Updated October 2, 2026.

What you share

The waitlist collects your email address, the time you join, and your consent to availability updates for an easier Spammish experience. It does not connect to Gmail or collect mailbox content.

Why we keep it

New entries are used only to announce availability of an easier Spammish experience. Existing Cloud-launch entries retain their original consent purpose; this update does not expand it. No marketing email is sent automatically by this website. Joining does not create an account or start a subscription.

Storage and access

Entries are stored privately on the site’s Fly.io storage volume. The list is not exposed through a public endpoint. Only the operator can access it. Fly.io may process request metadata to operate the site. We use a temporary hash of your network address in memory to limit abusive submissions; it is not saved with your email.

Your choices

To remove your entry, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile. Do not post your email in a public issue. We retain entries until you ask for removal or the applicable waitlist is retired.

Open-source app

The self-hosted application has its own privacy documentation. Joining does not promise a particular future format, price, date or Google approval. Any future service would need its own terms and privacy details.
